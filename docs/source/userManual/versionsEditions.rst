Raven Editions
--------------
The Raven MDI Platform is divided into two editions, the regular full edition and the community edition. The community edition is a lighter weight platform with only core features, omitting user management and testing event support. The community edition decreases the platform's footprint and streamlines presentation of features most relevant for the majority of users.
